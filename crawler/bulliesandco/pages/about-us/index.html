About Us

Bullies and Co. was created in 2017. We are a brand that is a small family owned business, with a limited quantity of dog chains.

Our vision was to create the highest quality dog collar that is affordable and can withstand the abuse of any animal.

Our luxurious dog collars are made for any size dog. Each collar is hand crafted, made out of medical grade 316L stainless steel, dipped in 18k gold, rose gold or silver finish, with our patented buckle jewelry style clasp and and has our beautifully engraved logo. You can easily attach an ID tag & leash. Our stunning gold dog collars magnify and compliment any dog's beauty.

We do our very best to manufacture the highest quality dog chains while giving our customers an affordable luxury product for their devoted fur friends. If you are seeking a collar that will make your dog stand out over the rest, get yours today!


 We welcome you with open arms to Bullies & Co! We hope you support our business and take this journey along with us! Thank you!
